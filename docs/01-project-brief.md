# Road Accessibility to Settlement Areas in Ibadan North LGA

## 1. The Question

Which settlement areas in Ibadan North Local Government Area, Oyo State, are farthest from the nearest mapped road?

## 2. Why It Matters

Road proximity can influence how easily people, services and resources can reach settled areas. Identifying settlement areas farthest from mapped roads can provide a simple spatial view of potential road-access gaps within Ibadan North LGA.

The question is deliberately limited to one Local Government Area so that the analysis can be completed, checked and reproduced within the project period.

## 3. The Data I Need

### Ibadan North Local Government Area Boundary

Used to define the study area and limit the analysis to Ibadan North LGA.

### GRID3 Nigeria Settlement Extents v4.1

Used to identify settlement areas within Ibadan North LGA.

### OpenStreetMap Road Network

Used to measure the distance from each settlement area to its nearest mapped road.

## 5. What I Would Build

I would build a spatial analysis that calculates the distance from each settlement area to its nearest mapped road. The final output would rank settlement areas by nearest-road distance and provide a map showing where the largest road-proximity gaps occur within Ibadan North LGA.

The analysis will use a projected coordinate reference system appropriate for the study area so that distances are calculated in metres.
